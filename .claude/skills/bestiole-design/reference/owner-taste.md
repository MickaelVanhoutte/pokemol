# What the owner loved and refused

Add a line after every review: what they said, and the rule it gives. Read this before every concept and every critic
brief.

## Loved (the bar: `tools/out/refs-sheet.png`)

- **Ombrouille** (« J'adore ombrouille ! »): a shadow with an attitude, one feature grown along the line (quiff, curls,
  flaming spikes).
- **Chapardou**, then **Pantharuse** once fixed: the cheeky thief with a stolen pencil in its mouth becomes the smug
  master thief sitting on its hoard. A story told by one prop and one pose.
- **Minousse**, **Minoufleur**: a sitting cat with a jagged leaf. **Bouquétigre**: a prowling tiger, the flower growing
  from its back, in front of the outline.
- **Braisou**, **Braisaure** (four-legged runner), **Dragobraise** (a flying fire serpent instead of a winged lizard).
- **Pschitou**, **Mouffetor** (« Nice »), **Lémurage**, the **Poupochette** kangaroo (kept as it was).
- **Clapotin** spinning on its back, its near legs lowered.
- **Clapoflot's posture**: diving nose first down the wave, rear lifting, speed lines (« j'aime bien la posture »).

## Standing preferences

- Animals, not objects. Cute first forms; finals impressive, never cluttered; legendaries real-shaped and dynamic.
- Keep each Bestiole's identity: few type changes, no accent of a type it doesn't have.
- The owner doesn't send sketches: « le but est que tu trouves une méthode ». Offer clearly different options instead.
- The owner is often on a phone: send images with SendUserFile, and review pages as Artifacts (no GIFs).
